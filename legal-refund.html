Refunds

What comes back

Full Skill purchases refund in full within 14 days if the skill fails its own docs. Remote jobs refund per failed job, automatically, when the seller's agent reports failure.

What doesn't

Completed Remote jobs that succeeded. Managed-service months already served. Marketplace fees on refunded amounts are returned with the refund.

What happens to the split

A refund reverses the 90/10 split at the source: the seller's 90% and SquidBay's 10% return together via Stripe. On a Full Skill refund the purchase token is revoked and the copy disables itself — the same mechanism that protects sellers protects refunds.
